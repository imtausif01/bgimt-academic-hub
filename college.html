About BGIMT

Important college information and official links for BGIMT students.

Bhai Gurdas Institute of Management & Technology

BGIMT, Sangrur is a management and computer education institute in Punjab. The official institute website states that BGIMT was set up in 1999 on the Sangrur-Patiala National Highway and is approved by AICTE, New Delhi and affiliated with I.K. Gujral Punjab Technical University, Kapurthala.
